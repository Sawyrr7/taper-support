Taper support

Quit weed or vaping gradually, at a pace your body can handle.

How does the taper work?

You tell Taper what you use and how much. It cuts your daily amount in even weekly steps, moves your first use later each week, and eases you down a little each day in the final week, reaching zero on your quit date.

What does it cost?

The first 7 days of your plan are free, and so are the optional measuring days before it. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks the full app for good. There’s no subscription. The craving toolkit and help lines always stay free.

I already bought it. How do I get it back?

Open Settings (tap your avatar, top right) → Taper Unlimited → Restore purchase. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I went over my limit. Now what?

That’s okay, and it doesn’t undo your progress. Taper asks what triggered it and lets you keep going or hold your current step for another week.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. Taper has no account and no server. Deleting the app or tapping “Reset my data” erases everything. See the privacy policy.

Reminders aren’t showing up

In Taper, open Settings → Notifications and turn on Daily check-in. Then make sure notifications for Taper are allowed in the iPhone Settings app → Notifications.
