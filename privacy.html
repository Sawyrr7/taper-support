Privacy policy

Taper: Quit Weed & Vaping · Effective November 1, 2026

The short version

Your plan, logs, check-ins and notes never leave your phone, and there is no account. To measure its ads, Taper uses Meta’s SDK, which tells Meta when the app is installed and opened and when a free trial or subscription starts. We don’t sell your data or track you across other apps.

What stays on your phone

Your plan, logs, cravings, triggers, check-ins, notes and settings are saved only on your device, inside the app. They are never sent to us, to Meta or to anyone else. Tap “Reset my data” in Settings, or delete the app, to erase them.

Moving to a new phone

If you tap “Export backup” in Settings, Taper creates a file with your data and hands it to the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it, such as AirDrop to your own new phone, and never to us. The file isn’t encrypted, so delete it once you’ve imported it.

Reminders

If you turn on notifications (morning plan, check-in, heads-ups, milestones), Taper writes them on your phone and iOS schedules them there. No server is involved.

Purchases

The optional subscription is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple Account information. The app only asks Apple whether this Apple Account has an active subscription (or bought the earlier one-time unlock). Meta is told when a trial or subscription starts, as described under Ad measurement.

Ad measurement (Meta)

Taper includes Meta’s Facebook SDK so Meta can tell us which of our ads led to installs. The SDK sends Meta: that the app was installed and opened, free-trial and subscription starts (with the price), an identifier created for this app on this device, and basic device details such as model and iOS version. It never receives your plan, logs, check-ins, notes or anything you type. Taper never asks to track you, so the SDK doesn’t use your device’s advertising identifier or follow you across other apps and websites. Meta handles this data under its own privacy policy.

Third parties

Apart from Meta’s SDK above, Taper includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. Tapping a help-line number or text link opens your phone’s dialer or Messages app. Those services are run by their own providers.

Children

Taper is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page and shown in the app before it takes effect.
